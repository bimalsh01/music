## Data that looks unreliable
- **Difficulty:** every market except US, India and Indonesia reports 0 for every keyword.
- **Indonesia:** 368 of 487 mapped keywords are English or exact US numbers; chord terms contradict each other ("chord piano" 40.5K vs "chord gitar" 170).
- **Russia:** 68 of 210 flagged; Russian terms have tiny volumes (метроном 1,000) because Google has a small share there.
- **Turkey:** most volumes sit at the 10/month floor; "tap tempo" repeats the US figure.
- **Grouped volumes:** close variants share one number (US "guitar tuner" / "tuner guitar" / "tune on guitar" all 301K; Italian "accordatore chitarra" variants all 60.5K). Each group counts once.
- **No market copies another's full set of numbers** (0 identical keyword volumes across markets except the flagged US copies).
